Privacy Policy — BC Roads & Frequencies

Last updated: October 26, 2026

BC Roads & Frequencies is a read-only reference app. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

No data collection

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

No network use

All road and frequency data ships inside the app and is read locally on your device. The app works entirely offline.

No device access

The app does not access your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone. Favourites you star are stored only in the app on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Optional location in a channel report

The app includes a "Report a wrong radio channel" form. If — and only if — you tap "Add my location," your device's coordinates and the current time are written into the email draft the form prepares on your device. Nothing is sent automatically. The report, including any location you added, is only ever sent if you choose to send that email yourself, and it goes only to the report inbox (bcroadreports@gmail.com) so the reported channel can be checked against the official district map.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of this, the policy will be updated here and in the app before that version is released.
