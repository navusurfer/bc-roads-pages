Support — BC Roads & Frequencies

BC Roads & Frequencies is an offline reference for British Columbia resource road radio channels: 1,197 roads and channel areas across all 28 Natural Resource Districts, each matched to its RR channel and frequency, compiled from the BC Ministry of Forests' official district channel maps and the ISED Appendix RR channel bank.

Contact

Questions, corrections, and support: bcroadreports@gmail.com

Reporting a wrong channel

The fastest way to report a channel that doesn't match the posted sign is the "Report a wrong radio channel" form inside the app (About tab, or the button on any road's detail screen). Reports are checked against the official district map, and confirmed corrections ship in app updates. Please note the form is for radio channels only — not road conditions, closures, or hazards.

A note on accuracy

Most BC resource roads are radio-assisted, not radio-controlled. This app is a planning and reference tool only — the channel posted on the sign at the road always governs, and maps can lag behind changes in the field. Transmitting on these frequencies requires an ISED mobile radio licence.

This is an independent reference app. It is not published by, affiliated with, or endorsed by the Government of British Columbia.
